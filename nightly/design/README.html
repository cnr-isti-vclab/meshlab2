Design Documents¶

Three kinds of document live here, and the distinction is worth keeping:

  • Reference (this directory) — how MeshLab works today. Read these to understand or change the code; correct them when the code changes.

  • Proposals — design settled before the code exists. Every one says “not implemented” in its opening paragraph. When one ships, its content moves into a reference document and the proposal is deleted, not left to rot.

  • History — dated records of work that is finished. They are evidence of how something came to be, not statements about the present. Records are never edited after the fact; if one turns out to be wrong, that is itself a fact about the record. Where a record’s conclusions are still in force, the rule lives in a reference document and the record only shows the reasoning.

Vocabulary is read by the test suite at runtime. test_filter_descriptors.cpp opens it by path and parses the ## 3. Verb lexicon section. Renaming the file or that heading breaks the build. Adding or retiring a verb there changes what the tests enforce, which is the intent.

Proposals¶

Document

Status

LLM Integration

Option space for driving MeshLab from a model. Not implemented.

Filter Representation

What a filter works on and produces (Mesh/Triangle, Point Cloud, …) as a classification facet beside the category; moved out of Vocabulary. Not implemented.

Usage Statistics

Aggregate usage collection in two channels: an update check that counts installs by version and platform without an identifier, and an opt-in session digest the census lets you reweight. Privacy by construction — no free text, no timestamps, a budgeted fingerprint, country counted unlinked. Not implemented.

Local Usage Model

The other half: a local journal of the user’s own actions, never transmitted, and the statistics built on it — search ranked by use, parameter defaults that learn, runtime estimates, next-filter and repeated-pipeline suggestions; the views that show a user their own record (session recap as a script, per-document provenance, comparison against the shipped prior) and four exports, including an air-gapped contribution bundle. Not implemented.

Gaussian Splatting

Splat loading, rendering and editing. Not implemented; some decisions taken, the renderer plan revised for Open3D 0.20’s MIT splat pipeline, and the core prerequisite (named attributes surviving undo) landed for float, Point3f and int.

Edge Support

Every stage has shipped; open questions only, ready for history. Stages 1-3 shipped 2026-09-16 (in history), stage 4, the polyline colorize, transfer and custom-attribute filters, 2026-11-26, and arc length along a chain, the edge option of Select All/None/Invert and the rubber band’s edge mode 2026-11-27.

Coral Branch Phenotyping

Mapping table for porting Yuri Andraccio’s curve-skeleton branch segmentation and measurement plugin (and its three vcglib additions, redone and landed 2026-09-28). Plugin not implemented; awaiting approval; curve skeleton approved, five issues open.

CinoLib Port

Nine proposed surface filters in three stages: UV mapping, polyharmonic fields, topology and polygon tools; source-verified integration constraints and a deferred-candidate survey. Not implemented.

Open3D RGB-D Reconstruction

RGB-D sequence reconstruction with Open3D (odometry, pose refinement, TSDF integration of raster layers with a depth plane), plus the Open3D candidates deliberately not taken (alpha shape, Poisson, persisting the pose graph). Not implemented; its share of the vendored Open3D code is not yet traced. Phases 0-2 shipped as filter_open3d and are in history.

Layer Selection Model

Merging visible and current into one selected set with a last-selected member, from the Tarini and Callieri sessions: the 136-reference mapping table, seven open decisions and a seven-step staging. Not implemented; awaiting approval of the table and the names.

Directional and Frame Fields

Ten proposed field synthesis, visualization, analysis, seamless parametrization and polygon remeshing filters, plus QuadWild and Instant Meshes consumers. Not implemented.

DiWR through QRhi Compute

Feasibility of porting the CUDA DiWR reconstructor (joint orientation, area weight and confidence for unoriented, noisy point clouds, extracted by weighted Screened Poisson) to QRhi compute: a measured source inventory, what CUDA, Thrust and cudaKDTree leave to rewrite, and a first experiment. Assessed 2026-11-15; not implemented, nothing approved.

TexMetro Reference Values

What remains of bringing TexMetro’s metrics into Measurement: Stage 0, checking our numbers against JSON written by TexMetro itself (it cannot run on a Mac), six questions for its authors and one about publishing the gargoyle atlases. The script and its --compare are ready. Not implemented; stages 1 to 5 shipped 2026-10-23 to 26 (see docs/texture_metrics.md) and are in history.

USD Support

Reading and writing USD (.usda, .usdc, .usdz) through LightUSD (formerly TinyUSDZ) as a pinned submodule, not OpenUSD, whose vcpkg port is shared-only: a flattened import first, as glTF’s, with UsdPreviewSurface mapped to MeshLab’s four material channels and upAxis/metersPerUnit handled; then scene I/O (Export Visible Layers as USD, layer matrices kept as prim transforms, and Import as Separate Layers); then one material-model extension shared with glTF. Library and flattened import decided 2026-11-23. Stage 0 done 2026-11-27: go, on LightUSD 1.0’s new next core and Tydra Next (the older core is deprecated). Stage 1 implemented 2026-11-27 (plugins/io_usd): flattened import and one-layer export, files pass all 30 OpenUSD validators; departures from the plan (faceVarying normals averaged, Y-up baked, centimeters by default) listed in §4.4. Stages 2 and 3 not implemented.

Load Options

Options an importer declares with the filter parameter schema (its third client after filters and preferences), resolved from defaults and remembered choices, recorded in the load_mesh action and in Python, kept on the layer so Reload repeats them. Opening never asks: the layer loads with the defaults and Reload with Options… in its context menu reads the file again with others. STL’s merge preference became the first option, io_vcg’s. Revives legacy MeshLab’s initPreOpenParameter without its dialog on every load. Stage 1 implemented (2026-11-28); options in the project are not.

LAS/LAZ Support

Reading and writing LAS and LAZ through LASzip (Apache-2.0, no dependencies), and a document origin kept in double precision, subtracted before the float conversion, so georeferenced coordinates survive: measured 2026-11-25, an E57 grid 1 mm apart at UTM coordinates collapses to 4 × 1 distinct positions. Classification and other fields as named attributes, a point budget by default, COPC level of detail later. The document origin is implemented for E57 (2026-11-28); LAS itself is not.

Citations to References

The 31 descriptions that typed their papers out, moved to the manifests’ references with Crossref evidence; three citations corrected, applied 2026-10-30.

Project Persistence

How the user’s work reaches the disk: a stored state for layers, textures and planes kept outside undo and shown as one mark in the layer panel; texture filters that write no file; Save Texture and Rename Texture; project save by stored state under unique names; the document folder. The 39-row mapping table and eleven decisions, applied 2026-10-31 in five stages.

Two-Layer Filters

The rule that a filter reading two layers and changing one changes the current layer: the 25 filters measured, the 15 migrated (five had written the other layer), Transfer Texture from Another Layer split out, five aligner and transfer renames, and selectionScopeMesh removed. Applied 2026-11-01 in three stages.

Face Scalar from Rasters

Turning Compute Face Scalar from Raster Coverage into a measure of how well a photo set covers each face: count, best resolution in pixels per unit, best angle; the majority-of-three-interior-points visibility rule; the 8-row mapping table, and the measured sensitivity of the absolute depth tolerance. Applied 2026-11-01.

Raster Alignment

Stage 3 of the lens model: old MeshLab’s three alignment plugins (10,846 lines, a 9-row table) replaced by about 1,700 lines with their comments, carried out in four stages: point correspondences with vcg::ShotResection, the drag-to-pair tool in the raster view, mutual information on a CPU rendering, and Dellepiane and Scopigno’s global refinement, ported from the paper where it and the old code disagree. The measurements behind each stage, the false maximum of the normal map on a real scan, and the nine decisions. Applied 2026-11-03.

Texture Consolidation

Liu, Wang, Kim and Kazhdan’s registration-and-stitching texture builder as a second Transfer Color from Visible Rasters to Texture, run as three helper programs from a recursive submodule on four forks. The measured upstream, the camera conversion and its test, two out-of-bounds reads and an uninitialized read that had corrupted every texture, the scheduling fixes that made the pipeline repeatable, the stitcher’s compile cost cut from 151 s to 57 s, and the move of the Linux build to Ubuntu 24.04. Completed 2026-11-07; what is still open, the upstream pull requests among it, is in plugins/filter_texture_consolidation/UPSTREAM.md.

Documentation Reorganization

The 139-section mapping table that gave each reference document one audience: UI behaviour to User Interface, third-party policy to its own document, finished migrations here, unbuilt designs to proposals, duplicates merged. Moves and trims applied 2026-11-15.

Folding the pymeshlab2 Wheel

Retiring the separate pymeshlab2 repository and building the wheel here: the 23-file mapping table, the in-app facade renamed from pymeshlab to pymeshlab2, vcpkg’s python3 port hijacking find_package(Python), and the wheels joining release.yml. Completed 2026-11-22 with the 2026.11 release; the old repository is archived.

Filter Search

The proposal behind word-start matching, one-way search aliases and five-level ranking, implemented 2026-10-30: the old search measured, the first alias table and the 24 reference queries. How it works now is in User Interface.

Orthographic Camera

The orthographic projection for the view and every camera consumer, in five stages (2026-10): the visible-height invariant, the 45-row mapping table, and the vcglib GetMatrix and GetFrustum fixes with their measurements. How it works now is in Rendering.

Camera Lens Model

Lens distortion behind a hard boundary, implemented 2026-11-02: vcglib’s old radial model and old MeshLab audited and removed (a 14-row table), the Brown-Conrady lens on rasters (an 11-row table), and native COLMAP import. How it works now is in Data Model.

Local Flattening

Pietroni et al.’s local flattening operator, steps 1 to 5, implemented 2026-11-15 to 17: why its equation 1 needs a conformal energy, the decisions, the PoissonSolver clean-up and the measurements on cylinders and scans. The rules are in vcglib’s local_flattening.h and the filter’s description.

Edge Support, Stages 1-3

The proposal as it stood when stages 1-3 shipped (2026-09-16): per-edge colour and scalar, the edge expression and colorize filters, selected edges in the overlay, stamped isocontour levels.

Open3D Scan Processing, Phases 0-2

The proposal behind filter_open3d (2026-10-14 to 10-30): the VTK build route rejected, the 116-file vendored subset and its three cuts, and the measurements behind the registration filters’ choices (FGR’s orientation limit, sequential chaining, restart, absperc lengths).

TexMetro, Stages 1-5

The case for CPU metrics and a deterministic texel rasterizer in vcglib over vendoring TexMetro or a QRhi raster, the measurements behind it, the 68-key mapping, the quirks of TexMetro’s code, and stages 1 to 5 as shipped 2026-10-23 to 26.

Texture Signal Processing Filters

The plan behind filter_texture_signal_processing’s nine filters (seam repair and dilation, smoothing and sharpening, inpainting, detail transfer, per-face curvature, line-integral convolution, reaction-diffusion), in process on a fork of TSP: the stage-0 memory and crash findings, the upstream pull requests, and Detail Size and Pattern Size as lengths. Implemented 2026-11-12 to 14; source-aware stitching not pursued.
