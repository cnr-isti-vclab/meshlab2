Citations moved to references, applied 2026-10-30¶

A filter’s papers belong in its manifest’s top-level references, attached with referenceIds (see adding_a_filter.md): the help citations, the [bib] / [doi] / [web] buttons and the generated references.bib are all built from them. 31 descriptions carried their citation as text instead, so none of that existed for them. This records the move.

How the citations were found¶

Every longDescriptionMarkdown was scanned for a year, “et al.”, a venue or journal name, a DOI, page or volume numbers, or words such as “paper”; the 36 hits were read one by one. Seven needed nothing: Instant Meshes (“by Jakob et al.”) and the texture consolidation filter (“by the method of Liu, Wang, Kim and Kazhdan (2026)”) already had their references and only name the authors in prose, and five more – the three Geogram parametrizations, Merge Texture Islands, Taubin smoothing (TrueForm) – matched on an ordinary word. A second pass for “Reference:” headings found the two bounding-box filters, whose citation has no year.

Evidence¶

Every entry was taken from its Crossref record, matched on title and authors, except two with none: Kazhdan 2006 (its Eurographics DOI resolves to the Eurographics Digital Library) and Takayama 2014 (JCGT issues no DOIs; the entry carries the journal’s URL). Crossref’s lowercased DOIs were given their usual capitals, its “Thürrner” corrected to Thürmer, and two titles it truncates or marks up restored (LoOP, Chang et al.).

Three papers were already declared in another manifest – bernardini1999 in filter_open3d, meyer2003discrete and crane2013heat in filter_igl – and were copied verbatim: docs/generate_api.py refuses a reference id defined two ways.

The three that are not plain moves¶

  • Scale space. The text cited Watertight Scenes from Urban LiDAR and Planar Surfaces (CGF 2013), and with its first two authors swapped (Crossref: van Kreveld, van Lankveld, Veltkamp). CGAL’s user manual credits the method to “Julie Digne et al. in 2011” (cgal:dmsl-ssmrp-11), which is Scale Space Meshing of Raw Data Point Sets, CGF 30(6), 10.1111/j.1467-8659.2011.01848.x.

  • Kinetic reconstruction. Kinetic Shape Reconstruction, TOG 39(5), 10.1145/3376918, is by Jean-Philippe Bauchet and Florent Lafarge. Sven Oesau, credited in the text, is an author of CGAL’s implementation (CGAL/Kinetic_surface_reconstruction_3.h), not of the paper.

  • Optimal bounding box. The text cited the CGAL manual chapter. CGAL’s own code says “The algorithm is based on a paper by Chang, Gorissen, and Melchior” (CGAL/Optimal_bounding_box/oriented_bounding_box.h): Fast Oriented Bounding Box Optimization on the Rotation Group SO(3,ℝ), TOG 30(5), 10.1145/2019627.2019641.

Guard¶

test_filter_descriptors.cpp now rejects a DOI link, or a line opening with “Reference:”, in a description: 16 of the 31 carried one. Prose naming the authors is left alone.
