Quick Help
3D View
Left drag Rotate trackball
Shift + Left drag Roll: turn the view about its axis, circling the centre
Click Make the layer under the cursor current
Double click Center trackball on picked point
Middle drag or Right drag Pan
Ctrl + Left drag Pan
Wheel Zoom
Ctrl + Wheel Move near clipping plane
Shift + Wheel Flatten the perspective, on to orthographic and back
5 Switch between perspective and orthographic
%RESET_CAMERA_SHORTCUT% Reset camera
%FRAME_SCENE_FROM_EYE_SHORTCUT% Frame the scene from here, turning and zooming only
UV View
Left drag Pan
Middle drag Pan
Wheel Zoom around cursor
Double click Recenter and zoom in
Layer Panel
Click eye Toggle layer visibility
Ctrl + Click eye Hide all other layers
Alt + Click eye Show all other layers
Shift + Click eye Invert all layer visibility
Ctrl + Click disclosure triangle Expand or collapse all layer details
Press F1 again to hide this help.
